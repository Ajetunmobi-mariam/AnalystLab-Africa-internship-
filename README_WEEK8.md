Health and Economic Growth Analysis Using World Development Indicators
## Project Overview
This project analyzes the relationship between economic growth and health outcomes using the World Development Indicators (WDI) dataset published by the World Bank. The analysis was conducted using Microsoft Power BI to explore trends in life expectancy, GDP per capita, GDP growth, and health expenditure from 2000 to 2024.
The project aims to identify patterns, compare countries and regions, and examine how economic performance influences health outcomes through interactive dashboards and data visualizations.
## Objectives
-Analyze global life expectancy trends

-Compare life expectancy across countries and regions.

-Examine GDP growth, GDP per capita, and health expenditure trends

-Investigate the relationship between GDP per capita, health expenditure, and life expectancy.

-insights using interactive Power BI dashboards

## Data Preparation
The following data cleaning and transformation steps were performed:

-Imported the WDI dataset into Power Query in Excel.

-Filtered the dataset to retain selected health and economic indicators.

-Promoted the first row as column headers.

-Unpivoted year columns into Year and Indicator Value.

-Converted Year to Whole Number and Indicator Value to Decimal Number.

-Removed duplicate records.

-Handled missing values by excluding incomplete records where necessary.

-Cleaned the Country table by removing records with missing regions.

-Created a relationship between the WDI Data and WDI Country tables using Country Code.

## Key Findings
-Global life expectancy showed an overall upward trend between 2000 and 2024.

-North America recorded the highest average life expectancy, while Sub-Saharan Africa recorded the lowest.

-Countries with higher GDP per capita generally experienced higher life expectancy.

-Increased health expenditure was generally associated with improved health outcomes.

-Significant disparities exist between regions in both economic performance and health indicators.

## Recommendations
-Increase investment in healthcare, particularly in regions with low life expectancy.

-Promote sustainable economic growth through policies that improve productivity and income levels.

-Improve access to quality healthcare services to enhance population health outcomes.

-Prioritize investments in lower-performing regions to reduce inequalities in health and economic development.

## Conclusion
This analysis demonstrates a positive relationship between economic development and health outcomes. Countries and regions with higher GDP per capita and greater investment in healthcare generally recorded longer life expectancy. The findings highlight the importance of sustained economic growth and strategic healthcare investment in improving quality of life and reducing regional disparities.
